WHY THIS BUILD WAS REBUILT

- Function-preservation audit confirms every named v0.18.3 function remains except the two intentionally removed Time Clock functions.

CUSTOMER BOOKING

BARBER DASHBOARD

BARBER MESSAGES

CLIENTELE + CLIENT TOOLS

OWNER / TONY WORKSPACES
